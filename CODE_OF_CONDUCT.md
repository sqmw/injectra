# Code of Conduct / 协作行为规范

We welcome respectful, constructive collaboration across backgrounds and experience levels.

- Discuss evidence and behavior; avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or threats.
- Respect privacy and access boundaries. Do not share another person's credentials, documents, conversations, or identifying information without permission.
- Distinguish measured findings, hypotheses, and planned work. Preserve negative results and acknowledge contributions.
- Keep review focused on the change. Explain disagreements and respond to corrections in good faith.

For a concern, contact the maintainer through an existing private collaboration channel. The maintainer may ask participants to revise/remove inappropriate material or restrict participation. Repository visibility and authorized access do not grant permission to redistribute private research or user data.

中文：尊重不同背景的协作者，讨论事实与证据，不进行攻击、骚扰或歧视。保护个人信息与研究材料，分清实测、推测与计划，保留失败结果并认可他人的贡献。争议通过已有私下协作渠道联系维护者处理。
